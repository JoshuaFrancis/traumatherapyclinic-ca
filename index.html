Clinical overview

Three trauma-specialised treatment streams.

01

EMDR and trauma processing

Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing delivered by EMDR-certified therapists for clients with single-incident and complex trauma histories. EMDR is among the most evidence-supported treatments for PTSD and trauma-related conditions, consistently outperforming talk therapy alone for trauma resolution. The clinic specialises in adapting EMDR protocols for clients with dissociative symptoms, attachment disruptions, and histories of developmental trauma requiring a stabilisation phase before active trauma processing begins.

02

Somatic and body-based therapy

Somatic experiencing, sensorimotor psychotherapy, and body-based trauma processing for clients whose trauma is held in the nervous system rather than accessible through cognitive approaches alone. Trauma stored in the body often does not respond to talk therapy, and somatic approaches address the physiological dysregulation that underlies hypervigilance, chronic tension, dissociation, and emotional flooding. Practitioners are trained in trauma-sensitive yoga, breathwork, and body-awareness practices as adjuncts to clinical treatment.

03

Intensive outpatient programmes

Multi-day intensive trauma treatment for clients who have the capacity and motivation to pursue accelerated healing rather than conventional weekly therapy. Intensive formats compress months of standard treatment into focused multi-session blocks, achieving trauma resolution in weeks for clients who are stable enough to tolerate a higher therapeutic dose. Programmes are designed for clients travelling to access specialised care, professionals with inflexible schedules, and individuals for whom the standard therapy cadence has not produced progress.
